## Instructions
- Open the application in Android Studio or a compatible phone via Android Studio's connection functionalities (Pull this code in to your IDE)
- Sign up & log in with a valid FSC Email Address to The Herd
- Engage in The Herd's social features!
- For further description of the application's features, see the Features section

## Description
  The Herd is a Android OS social media app for FSC students to connect more easily with their peers. Due to the nature of a commuter school, it’s clear that many Farmingdale students feel that they have trouble finding people they can easily relate to throughout their entire time here. This problem extends to all students, be they commuters, dorming students, or transfers of any class level. The environment just provides a lot of friction that The Herd seeks to ease with a number of features targeting all student groups. The primary function of The Herd is to connect students, our users, by providing them with an app that lets them add hobbies and interests in the form of Topics to their profile and then connect users who share these hobbies and interests with one another. The Herd will be an app specifically for active Farmingdale students, requiring school email for sign up verification to ensure that the only users on the app are real people who are accessible, people who you can actually become friends with. Exceptions may be made for alumni who engage in the mentorship feature for up to a year, or students who need to stop attending college and then return at a later date. Each Topic provides users with a space to engage with each other with a variety of different features such as viewing existing posts and comments in the Community Boards, creating and RSVPing to events (on campus or online) organized by students within a topic, creating new posts, commenting on existing posts, and liking other user’s comments within a Post. There will be search functionality to filter and find topics (such as hobbies or interests), and if the search yields no results the user will be provided with the ability to create a new Topic & its respective features altogether.

  Since The Herd is a social media application specifically for connecting students with the intention of helping form real life friendships, there are functional friend lists for users to stay in contact with one another. Here a user may add or delete friends via sending friend requests that must be accepted by the other party, accessible from another student’s profile or through a student's email or username. Once added, students may engage outside of the Community Boards with other features such as direct messaging, viewing each other’s profiles, event sharing, creating “virtual” goal commitments, such as two users agreeing to commit to a study or gym session together, and viewing status updates of their friends. If students desire, they can block or unblock users they no longer wish to engage with. Students who commit to particular tasks repeatedly may even go on a “success streak” and level up for achieving their goals when working on their commitments whether they're alone or with friends. Additionally, there is a volunteer mentorship/mentee program for students to engage in & provide valuable advice and insights they may have wished they had when they first arrived at FSC.

 Since college can be a stressful time, especially for freshmen, there are a number of features that cater to students who might be struggling with stress or the adjustment process. Beyond the volunteer mentorship/mentee program, there are a number of helpful “Campus Life” guides that aim to help ease the pain and frustration of tasks that are not explained by the school in great detail. These guides will include topics such as shuttle use, WiFi connection, Financial Aid, logging in to school computers, and more by attempting to address pain points for students on campus. Students may also create their own guides for others to help fill the gaps left in the system. If a guide is unclear, a student may ask questions on the guide or answer questions left by other students. To answer these questions in a guide's Q&A section, the community can come together to vote on the most helpful answer for display on the main guide page. The motivation feature focuses on providing encouragement to uplift students with motivational reminders. The Events feature displays the events a student has RSVP’d to, and provides them with a means to share events they’re interested in with Friends to have a more successful turnout.
